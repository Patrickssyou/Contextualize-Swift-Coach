## 3. What v2 changed

1. **Scale is 0–3 + NA.** 0 = not done or made it worse, 1 = poor, 2 = acceptable, 3 = excellent. Every criterion has an NA option with its own condition. NA is left out of the total: the weight is redistributed across the criteria that applied. ("Mark 3 if not applicable" is gone.)
2. **Reasons are required** for any score below 3, and for every score (including NA) on 2b, 2c and 2d.
3. **Case facts (answer key):** root cause, fault (agent said vs actual), error type (product / process / both), action taken, promised to client, follow-up owner, resolved without another team (single touch), and what the auditor checked to know this (call recording, screen recording, Greendot, WIRA, MGR tool, DE tool, Slack, Lightdash, CMA, other).
4. **Gut call** (Good / OK / Bad) is required. The running score stays hidden until it's picked, so the gut call isn't anchored. Good maps to ≥90, OK to 75–89, Bad to <75. Disagreements are shown in Results.
5. **Required call details:** call link, restaurant ID, call date and reviewer. The restaurant ID (R#####) and callId are read from the Greendot link automatically.
6. **Week** comes from the queue item's week, otherwise from the Monday of the call date. The header week no longer decides where an audit is filed.
7. **Rubric versioning:** every audit stores `rubric: "v2"`. The 5 audits saved before v2 have no `rubric` field and are treated as v1. They stay in the db and the export, are left out of v2 results, and are listed under "Rubric v1 audits" with Re-score and Delete. Re-score opens a v2 form prefilled with the call and stores `replaces: <v1 id>`.
8. **AE audits:** "AE…" chip with a name field (`role: "ae"`). Results show Super Agents vs AEs when any AE audit exists.
9. **Type switch bug fixed:** switching interaction type with scores entered asks for a second click, then clears scores and reasons. They used to carry over silently.
10. **Smaller rubric fixes:** the 2c descriptors now describe the action taken rather than knowledge. CL call/chat 1d no longer includes "communicated the resolution clearly", which 3c already scores. MGR/DE 1d keeps it because MGR/DE has no closing criterion. CL chat 1c says "difficult chats".
11. **Queue:** the weekly sample (section 4) fills it automatically. Pasting from Wonka still works under "Add calls by hand": it detects type from a Wonka cell (CL, MGR, DE, "DE MGR", chat) and the call date from the row (earliest date in the row wins), stores restaurant ID and callId, and keeps paste order (`pos`). "Audits done" uses each agent's queued count as the target, or 5 when nothing is queued.
12. **Results:** team average, audits done, fault accuracy, single touch, gut vs score; per agent: Communication / Investigation / Resolution averages, fault accuracy and weakest criterion; week on week; where the team loses points (with NA rate); gut-vs-score disagreements; the week's audits with fault (agent → actual).
13. **Export:** one workbook with three tabs (section 8), via Download .xlsx or Export to Google Drive.
14. **Copy:** helper subtext trimmed per Patrick's preference (no keyboard tip, no save hint, fewer section sub-labels). Keyboard: 0–3 score, N = NA, ↑/↓ move, Enter in a reason jumps to the next criterion that needs work. After saving a queued call, the agent's next unscored call loads.

## 4. Weekly sample and linked calls (v2.1, v2.2)

A normal week is 5 queries.

Using the chain rather than the single leg keeps short legs that were part of a longer case, such as a dropped call followed by a callback.
- **Linked calls = one big call.** Patrick's model: the calls linked to the sampled call are treated as one interaction, appended in time order, and the audited Super Agent is graded only on their own calls in it. Everyone else in it is context. Later, the AI (Swift Coaching) gets the same ordered parts from the `linked_calls` export tab.

- **Chaining:** start from the sampled call and keep adding in-scope calls that are within 10 minutes of the linked call so far (measured from the last hang-up, or to the first call's start) and within 60 minutes of the sampled call, when:
  1. the audited agent handled it for a restaurant already in the linked call, on any number (store MGR calls);
  2. it was to or from a phone number already in the linked call (callbacks, transfers, a CRT or CSR leg on the same caller, another Super Agent picking the case up);
  3. it's an internal call from the audited agent (the other side shows the agent's login as the number).
  Repeat until nothing new joins. Each part stores who handled it (roster name or Lightdash full name), their group (graded, sa, pool, crt, csr), direction, the other party (caller, …last 4 digits, or an agent), length, and whether it came before the sampled call. ## 4b. Linked WIRA tasks and "What Claude got" (v2.4)

**Task linking** (Lightdash explore `wira_tasks`; rules in `core.js` → `linkTasks`). A task belongs to a case when it was created from 10 min before the first linked call to 20 min after the last one ended, and (1) it is on the case's restaurant (`wira_tasks_restaurant_unique_code`), or (2) the graded agent created it and it has no valid restaurant code (or the case has none), or (3) its `conversation_id` is one of the calls (always null today). Projects `real-time-audits-pizza` (QA audits) and `customer-feedback` (automated SMS) are skipped.
- Why: most `live-support-cos-self-resolved` tasks have no creator recorded (Angel's T1781719 included), so restaurant + time is the main key. The Super Agents' own named tasks land 0.5–8 min after the call ends.
- Each task is flagged `mine` (graded agent created it; null when no creator), `commented` (agent in the commentors), `mentioned` (@agent in description/comments), and `when` before/during/after with minutes and the call number.
- Creator strings per agent are in `meta/roster.agents[].creators` (e.g. velkapassionwonderscocom, marlaangelalacsamanawondersco, "Angel Lacsamana").
- WIRA link: `https://greendot.prod.letsdowonders.io/greendot/wira/tasks/{number without T}` (pattern seen in a task comment; untested by Patrick).
- The pull scans tasks (light columns, split at the 1,000-row cap), links, then fetches full fields for linked ids only. If the task lookup fails, calls still queue with `tasks: null`, and the score form offers "Find tasks".
- Sep 21–27: 24 tasks across 17 of the 30 sampled calls (backfilled into the live queue on Oct 2). The after-window was widened from 10 to 20 min on Patrick's call (artifact Version 7): it added Velka's C100 task (+18 min) and CJ's C56 engineering ticket (+15 min), nothing unrelated.

**What Claude got.** Under Case facts, a panel shows Claude's read of the case in Patrick's own fields, hidden until he clicks Show (to avoid anchoring). ## 4c. Calls vs cases, voicemail filter, Slack (v2.6)

- **Talked filter.** Each leg's transcript is classified: `voicemail` (greeting phrases like "forwarded to voicemail", "leave a message", "after the tone", and under 400 chars), `silent` (under 40 chars) or `talk`. Only `talk` legs count for the chain, timeline, case grouping and the call count. The others are folded behind "Show N voicemail / no-answer calls". Call status from Lightdash can't do this (nearly every leg is "finished").
- **Call vs case.** A pick is a **call** when only the sampled agent talked to the caller. It's a **case** when another SA, an SA pool seat or CRT also talked on a leg. Picks whose talked legs overlap are merged into one case (union-find), so Velka's and Brix's Today's Pizza picks are one case, graded once.
- **Queue.** Case picks carry a CASE tag and are listed in a Cases panel with a "Score case" button. Week of Sep 28: 30 picks, 10 case picks forming 8 cases.
- **Case facts.** "Who did what": one row per teammate with Owned it / Helped, "Told the caller the fault was" (per person), and a note. A case owner is required to save. This answers fault attribution when several SAs handled it.
- **Case rubric (`V2.case`, draft).** Diagnosis: 1a root cause and fault agreed across the team (.20), 1b facts gathered once (.10). Teamwork: 2a owner and handoffs (.15), 2b Slack/WIRA coordination (.15), 2c one voice to client and customer (.10). Resolution: 3a fix and promises closed (.20), 3b ticket (.05), 3c no loops (.05). Reason always required on 1a and 3a.
- **Slack.** "Find threads" searches Slack (restaurant code, then the quoted name) from 2 h before the first call to 8 h after the last, reads each thread, and keeps up to 5 that involve a teammate or sit in an operations/support/escalation channel. Threads are saved to every queue doc in the case and on the audit, and are fed into "What Claude got". Velka's Today's Pizza case is pre-loaded with the two #client_operation_assist threads (C9 remake and C24).
- **Results and export.** The agent table uses call audits only; cases get their own table. New audit columns: case_owner, case_team, slack_threads. New sheet `case_team` (member, role, told_caller_fault, said_matches_actual). `linked_calls` gains `talked`.

## 6. Scoring

- **v2:** each scored criterion earns `score ÷ 3 × weight`; NA criteria are left out. Total = earned ÷ weight of applicable criteria × 100. Sections and dimensions are computed the same way.
- **v1 (kept for the 5 old audits):** `(score − 1) ÷ 2 × weight`; N/A = full marks.
- **Bands:** ≥90 strong, 75–89 watch, <75 coach. Claude set these for the 1–3 scale; they may need re-tuning for 0–3, where a straight-2 call scores 66.7.
